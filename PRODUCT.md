# Product

## Register

product

## Users
Researchers, graduate students, and knowledge workers who read papers continuously and need a local-first workspace to collect sources, extract evidence, annotate PDFs, and turn AI output into reusable notes instead of disposable chat history.

## Product Purpose
LitFolio turns paper discovery, reading, and AI assistance into a long-lived knowledge workflow. The product exists to help users move from "find and ask" to "import, verify, synthesize, and retain". Success means every useful AI action can become a durable asset in the user's library, with clear source grounding and low-friction retrieval later.

## Brand Personality
Quiet, rigorous, durable. The tone should feel like a serious research tool: concise, reliable, and grounded in evidence rather than novelty theater.

## Anti-references
- Generic chatbot shells where output disappears into a scrolling transcript
- Dashboard-heavy SaaS styling that prioritizes decoration over reading and evidence
- Magic-looking AI flows that hide source provenance or silently degrade on failure

## Design Principles
- Preserve research flow: importing, reading, asking, and writing should feel like one continuous workspace.
- Make outputs durable: useful AI results should be savable, revisitable, and easy to fold back into the library.
- Keep evidence visible: conclusions should stay connected to source papers, snippets, and user highlights.
- Prefer local clarity over AI spectacle: interfaces should support focused work, not perform intelligence.
- Let tasks route cleanly: different AI jobs can use different models, languages, and levels of cost.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
Target clear contrast, keyboard-friendly primary actions, readable density for long sessions, and motion used only for state changes. Avoid relying on color alone to convey status, and keep critical evidence and save states visible in text.
